Our story

A calm corner in Birendranagar

Shulabh is a bakery in the morning, a restaurant by noon and a cafe all evening. Bread and pastries come out of our oven through the day, and every plate from the kitchen is cooked when you order it.

The room is bright and easy, with warm lamps, green plants and a marble counter where the coffee is ground fresh. Friends stay for hours, and some evenings someone brings a guitar.
